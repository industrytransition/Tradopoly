What is this?

Heavy industry = 30% of global CO2. But what if we make decarbonisation profitable through trade? Players build a green industrial system across 6 corridors — from Coal Mine ($60) to Hydrogen Export Terminal ($360).

1. Fossil
Coal $60, Oil & Gas $80, Fossil Power $100 — cheap but risky
2. Utility
Scrap Supply $120, Water $140, Renewable Grid $180 — +1 DP
3. Circular
Scrap Processing $160, Battery $220, Heat $240, Symbiosis $300 — +1 DP
4. Trade
Bilateral $120, Strategic $260, Green Corridor $340 — +1 Trade Token
5. Steel
EAF $200, DRI $260, HBI $320 — +1 DP
6. Hydrogen
Electrolyser $220, Storage $280, Export $360 — +DP/Token

GO = National Green Industrial Strategy: +$200 +1 Token when you pass. Carbon Lock-in = Jail: Pay $100 or miss turn. Transition Chance & Development Finance cards are shuffled (12 each) — CBAM, concessional finance, cost overruns, etc. Original educational game, not affiliated with Hasbro MONOPOLY®.
