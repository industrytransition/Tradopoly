📜 How to Play — Rules, DP, Tokens, Winning

Goal: Highest FINAL SCORE after 30 mins or when all properties bought. FINAL = Cash + Tokens×$100 + Decarbonisation Points (DP)×$200

  • Start: Everyone at GO, $500. Pass GO: +$200 +1 Token (Trade Token)
  • Roll: Only player whose turn it is can roll. Auto-ends after action.
  • Properties: 20 buyable. Each corridor: Fossil (brown), Utility (blue), Circular (green), Steel (grey), Trade (gold), Hydrogen (teal). Buying gives DP (decarbonisation) or Tokens if marked.
  • Rent: If you land on opponent's property, you pay rent shown. Rent goes to owner — visible in log and player card.
  • Transition Chance (❓): Policy shocks — CBAM, green procurement, tech breakthrough. Can give cash, DP, Tokens, or take money. Card text explains.
  • Development Finance (💰): Grants, concessional loans, guarantees. Can give +$100, +DP, +Token, free jail exit, etc.
  • DP: Earned from buying green assets (Utility, Circular, Steel, Hydrogen) and from cards. 1 DP = $200 at end. Tokens = $100. Tokens represent trade agreements / market access.
  • Jail = Carbon Lock-In: Land on it → pay $100 to exit or miss 1 turn.
  • Winning: Strategy: Diversify corridors, collect rent, stack DP. Trade corridor gives Tokens, Hydrogen/Steel gives high DP.

Turn flow: Roll → Move → If property unowned → BUY ($price) or SKIP → Auto next player. If owned → Pay rent → Auto next. If Chance/Finance → Draw card → Apply → Auto next. No manual END TURN needed.
