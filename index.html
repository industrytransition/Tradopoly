How to Play — Detailed

Final Score: Cash + Trade Tokens×$100 + DP (Decarbonisation Points)×$200. Highest wins.

Trade Tokens: Renamed from Tokens. Earned when: 1) You pass GO (+1), 2) You buy Trade Corridor assets (Bilateral, Strategic, Green Corridor, H2 Export) — each gives +1 Trade Token, 3) You land on Industrial Partnership Hub and do a Trade (Option A or B) — you get Trade Tokens, 4) From Chance/Finance cards.

Industrial Partnership Hub — Trade Options:
Option A — Trade a Property: Swap one of your properties with another player's property (both must agree in real life — game just records swap). You both get +1 Trade Token for trading.
Option B — Share a Property: Choose one of your properties to share. You keep ownership but you give another player shared access. You get +1 Trade Token immediately, and rent is split 50/50 when others land. (Simplified: sharer gets rent half).
If you don't want to trade, take +1 Trade Token as partnership bonus.

Transition Chance (Blue?): Detailed cards:

  • CBAM Shock: EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism hits. If you own Steel (EAF/DRI/HBI) you get green premium +$150 +1 Trade Token. If not, no bonus. Teaches CBAM impact.
  • Green Public Procurement: Govt mandates green steel. You get +$60 procurement payment.
  • H2 Demand Surge: Hydrogen demand spikes. +$100. If you own any Hydrogen (Electrolyser/Storage/Export) +1 DP extra.
  • Circular Economy Mandate: EU mandates recycled content. If you own Circular (Scrap Processing/Battery/Heat/Symbiosis) +$75 per Circular asset you own.
  • Tech Breakthrough — DRI: Breakthrough in green DRI cuts costs. +$120 +1 DP (Decarbonisation Point).
  • Trade Mission: You join green trade mission to Global South. +$80 +1 Trade Token.
  • Fossil Price Spike: Oil spikes. If you own Fossil (Coal/Oil/Fossil Power) you collect $25 from each other player.
  • Carbon Cost: If you still own Fossil, pay $30 carbon cost. No fossil = no cost (incentive to divest).

Development Finance (Green Bank): Detailed:

  • Concessional Loan: Low-interest loan for green project. +$100 cash.
  • Decarb Grant: Grant for Steel or Hydrogen decarbonisation. If you own EAF/DRI/HBI or Electrolyser/Storage/Export, +$150.
  • Export Credit: Trade finance. If you own Trade Corridor or H2 Export, +1 Trade Token (represents market access).
  • Blended Finance: Mix grant+private if you own any green (Utility/Circular/Steel/H2). +$70 +1 DP.
  • Cost Overrun: Project costs more. Pay $50.
  • Skills Grant: Workforce training for green jobs. +$100.
  • De-risking Guarantee: Free exit from Carbon Lock-In Jail next time. Save card.
  • Feasibility Study Bonus: Small grant +$40 and good luck next turn.
